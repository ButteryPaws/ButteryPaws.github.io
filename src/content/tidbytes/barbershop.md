I recently had one of my worst barbershop experiences and my asymmetrical beard is a testament to that. Somewhere in between, I had the following conversation after having established 5 minutes ago that I couldn't speak Tamil:

> Barber (in Hindi): So, where are you from?\
> Me (in Hindi): I am from Goa\
> Barber (in Hindi): Oh…\
> Barber (in Hindi): So, dont they speak Tamil in Goa?\
> Me: 😳\
> Me (slowly, in Hindi): _Uhh, no_

The worst part? The dude wasn't even Tamil himself, he was talking to the other barbers there in a Bhojpuri-Hindi hybrid.
