The trick to getting a Rapido (bike-taxi) faster is to change your name on the app to a female name. 
I use a gender-neutral name rather than an explicitly female name (take your pick from Kiran, Sashi, Madhu etc). It does give me the occasional "Did you book this for yourself?" or the unspoken question of suspicion, but I feel like my cancellation numbers are much better than others. 

This is one of my original life hacks. I love to believe it works and plan to do A/B testing some day to validate my belief. 

The downside, though, is probably lower reviews from an upset captain who just got baited-and-switched (bait-and-switched?).
