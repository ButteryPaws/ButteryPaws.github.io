The Trilemma of Monetary Molicy says that it is impossible to have all three at the same time:
 * A fixed foreign exchange rate
 * Free capital movement
 * An independent monetary policy
A central bank can choose at most 3 (or in my country, zero).

The CAP Theorem or the Trilemma of Distributed Systems says that any distributed data store can guarantee at most two of these three guarantees
 * Consistency
 * Availability
 * Partition Tolerance

In this spirit, I would like to propose my own theory, the Trilemma of Housing. It states as follows:
> "You can rent a house which will provide you at most two of the following three comforts"
> * Proximity to the place of work
> * Affordability of rent
> * Liveliness and livability of the surrounding area
